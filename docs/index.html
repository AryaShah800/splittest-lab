SplitTest Lab: should Cookie Cats move its first gate to level 40?

A/B test of 90,189 Cookie Cats players (public Kaggle dataset, retrospective analysis, not pre-registered). Decision rule written before the analysis.

-0.82 pp
Moving the gate to level 40 cut 7-day retention from 19.02% to 18.20% (95% CI -1.33 to -0.31 pp, p = 0.0016).

Action: Do not ship. Keep the gate at level 30, as the pre-written decision rule in brief.md requires.

Impact: A 1.6% to 7.0% relative drop in the share of players still active after a week.

820 players
Per 100,000 new players, gate_40 would leave about this many fewer active on day 7 (simple arithmetic on the point estimate).

Action: Use this as the cost side when weighing any monetisation upside the data cannot show.

Impact: Plausible range 312 to 1,328 per 100,000 (from the confidence interval). Revenue was not measured.

-1.09 pp
The loss concentrates in heavy players (more than the median rounds): -1.09 pp, p = 0.0151; light players show -0.01 pp (p = 0.96).

Action: If a later gate is revisited, look at engaged players first. Treat this as a lead, not a finding.

Impact: Rounds are measured after assignment, so the segment is descriptive only.

SRM p = 0.0086
Group sizes are 44,700 vs 45,489, a sample-ratio warning, and the placebo group (under 30 rounds) also differs (-0.33 pp, p = 0.049).

Action: Ask how players were assigned before trusting any follow-up test; rerun with a balanced split.

Impact: The result deserves slightly less trust than a perfectly balanced test. It passes the pre-set stop threshold (p < 0.001).

0.73 pp detectable
The test could detect a 7-day gap of about 0.73 pp (80% power). 1-day retention (p = 0.074) and game rounds (p = 0.0502) are not significant or only borderline.

Action: In the next test add revenue and longer-term retention as metrics and size it for a smaller effect.

Impact: The observed 0.82 pp gap sits just above the detectable limit, so the finding is real but with little margin.

Read with care. Mild sample-ratio warning (p = 0.0086) and a borderline placebo difference suggest something besides the gate may contribute. Retention only: no revenue was available.
